Prototype v0.2. On a phone: turn it sideways, joystick to move, swipe to look, tap the name card to switch hunters. Terrain: real elevation (USGS 3DEP) and aerial photos (USGS NAIP), both public domain. Roads, rivers and buildings © OpenStreetMap contributors. Characters and animations: Quaternius (CC0). Crew HQ is the house off Highway 28. Elk country is the upper Bohannon Creek drainage (best guess for Eagle Valley). All lines are made-up parody. Hunting rules here are game mechanics, not real hunting advice.
